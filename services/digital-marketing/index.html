Why Scientific Growth Marketing Outperforms Vanity Metrics

In digital marketing, high visitor impressions mean very little if they do not convert into qualified client relationships and verifiable revenue. Many campaigns waste ad spend bidding on broad, ambiguous keywords with zero commercial intent or driving traffic to unoptimized, high-friction landing pages.

At Baadam Info and Tradelink Services, we approach digital marketing with analytical rigor and technical precision. We combine foundational technical SEO audits, high-intent Google Ads management, precision social advertising, and rigorous Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) to build self-sustaining customer acquisition engines for your business.

Technical SEO Visibility

Rigid semantic crawling, schema markup, and speed audits that position your business at the top of organic commercial searches.

Targeted Intent Capture

PPC search and social campaigns engineered specifically to capture buyers actively searching for your solutions right now.

Transparent Attribution

Clean GA4 server-side tracking and multi-touch reporting that proves exact acquisition costs and channel profitability.

[ DELIVERY FRAMEWORK ]

Our Six-Step Growth Marketing Process

A methodical six-phase lifecycle designed to uncover growth bottlenecks, test hypotheses, and scale profitable channels.

PHASE 01 01

Audit & Competitor Benchmarking

Auditing technical website health, backlink strength, historical ad efficiency, and reverse-engineering competitor traffic sources.

PHASE 02 02

High-Intent Keyword & Audience Mapping

Identifying high-intent transactional search keywords, long-tail search opportunities, and customer demographic segments.

PHASE 03 03

Tracking & Landing Page Setup

Configuring server-side Google Tag Manager, GA4 conversion events, Meta Pixel, and creating high-converting dedicated campaign landing pages.

PHASE 04 04

Campaign Launch & Content Rollout

Deploying segmented Google Ads search campaigns, targeted social ad creative, and publishing pillar SEO content across search engines.

PHASE 05 05

Attribution & Performance Analysis

Analyzing real search term reports, cost-per-lead trends, click-through rates, and tracking customer drop-off points along the sales funnel.

PHASE 06 06

Iterative Scaling & Conversion CRO

Reallocating budget to highest-yielding channels, running ongoing A/B landing page tests, and expanding into profitable adjacent keywords.
